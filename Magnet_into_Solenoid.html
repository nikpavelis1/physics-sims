How to predict it

  1. Lenz's law: the coil opposes the motion. A magnet moving towards A is repelled, so end A becomes the same pole as the one approaching. A magnet moving away is attracted, so A becomes the opposite pole.
  2. Right-hand grip rule: point your thumb along the coil towards its North end; your fingers curl the way the current flows. Then look into end A to name it clockwise or anticlockwise.
